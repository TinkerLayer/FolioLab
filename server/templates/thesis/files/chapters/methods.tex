\chapter{Methods}
\label{ch:methods}

We model the quantity of interest as
\begin{equation}
  y(t) = A\left(1 - e^{-t/\tau}\right) + \varepsilon,
  \label{eq:model}
\end{equation}
where $\tau$ is a time constant and $\varepsilon$ is noise~\cite{mittelbach2004}.
